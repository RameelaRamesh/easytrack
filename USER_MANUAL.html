1. Platform Overview & Architecture

EasyTrack is an enterprise Multi-Tenant Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) application designed specifically for Medical Billing Workforce & Operations Management. It combines workforce lifecycle management (HRMS), daily claim batch execution, client SLA monitoring, quality assurance auditing, and financial payroll processing into a single integrated platform.

Core Architectural Pillars

1. Multi-Tenancy Isolation: Data isolation is enforced at the database query level via TenantModelViewSet. Every company's data is isolated so no tenant can ever view another's records.
2. Single Active Device Session: Prevents credential sharing and protects confidential Protected Health Information (PHI). If a user logs in from a second device, the previous session is immediately revoked with a "Session Superseded" notification.
3. Privacy-First Messaging: 1-on-1 and team chats are restricted strictly to conversation participants. Executives cannot read private chats they are not part of, and audit logs record only metadata (timestamp and actors), never message content.
4. Resilient Backend: Powered by Django REST Framework + PostgreSQL in production, with seamless fallback to SQLite for local development.

3. Authentication & Account Management

EasyTrack provides a secure, streamlined authentication and credential lifecycle workflow:

  • Organization Registration (/register): A 3-step wizard allowing new healthcare providers or billing companies to create their tenant organization, configure timezone/currency, set standard working days, and provision the CEO account.
  • Role-Based Login (/login): Features category switchers for Organisation (CEO, Ops Head, TL), HR, and Employee with show/hide password toggles.
  • Password Recovery (OTP): Users enter their registered corporate email address to receive an instant One-Time Password (OTP) with mandatory password reset on next login.
  • Single Active Device Session: Re-logging in from a different browser or machine instantly terminates the prior session with a security banner to protect PHI data.
  • Access Control Modal: Administrators and HR Managers can provision credentials for staff with auto-generated passwords, role assignment, and login activation.

5. Executive Suite: CEO Portal

Accessible at /ceo-dashboard. Designed for strategic oversight, financial health, and governance:

  • Executive Telemetry Cards: Total Headcount, Active Staff, Attendance Rate for selected date, Monthly Base Payroll, Target Incentives Earned, and Active Client Projects.
  • Business Portfolio Hub (/portfolio): Clients, Projects, and Financial burn rate vs projected revenue.
  • Roles & Permissions Matrix (/roles-permissions): Fine-grained toggle of View, Create, Edit, Delete, Approve, and Export permissions across all 19 modules.
  • Audit Logs & Revert (/audit): Complete tamper-evident log of all system actions with one-click action reversal.

6. Operations Head Workspace

Accessible at /operations-dashboard. Manages operational delivery, batch allocation, and client SLAs:

  • Clients Management (/clients): Create clients, assign Ops Heads and TLs, maintain contact details, and trigger operational check-in emails.
  • Processes & SOPs (/processes): Configure standard billing workflows (Prior Auth, Claims, Denial Resolution), set daily quota targets, and maintain payer-specific SOP manuals.
  • Work Queues & Batch Allocation (/billing): Assign work batches to Team Leads and billing executives with target quantities, priority ratings, and due dates.
  • Escalations & SLA Management (/escalations): Review and resolve client escalations and investigate operational blockers.

7. Team Lead (TL) Portal

Accessible at /tl-dashboard. Coordinates daily team execution and workforce scheduling:

  • Shift Roster Scheduling (/scheduling): Assign Day (08:00–17:00), Evening (14:00–23:00), or Night (22:00–07:00) shifts to team members.
  • Task Kanban Board (/tasks): Track team tasks across To Do, In Progress, QA Review, and Completed stages.
  • Attendance Oversight: Monitor punch times, active breaks, and investigate missed punches.
  • Leave Recommendations: Review employee leave requests and add recommendation notes before HR approval.

8. Human Resources (HR) Management Suite

Accessible at /hr-dashboard, /talent, and /time-payroll:

  • Recruitment Pipeline (/recruitment): Manage job openings and advance candidate cards through Applied, Screening, Interview, Selected, and Offer Released stages with one-click conversion to employee.
  • 6-Step Onboarding Clearance: Verify Personal Info, Position, Education, Bank Details, Document Checklist (ID, Marks, PAN, Certifications), and Declaration Sign-off.
  • Central Leave Management: Review and approve/reject Casual, Sick, Earned, and Loss of Pay (LOP) leaves.
  • Overtime Engine: Approve logged overtime hours with automated 1.5x and 2.0x rate multipliers.
  • Monthly Payroll Processing: Calculate net salaries, lock the payroll cycle, generate digital payslips, and export bank disbursement CSV files.
  • 4-Department Exit Clearance (/offboarding): Coordinate Asset, HR, Security/IT, and Finance clearances during employee offboarding.

9. Employee Self-Service & Daily Operations

Accessible at /employee-dashboard, /my-desk, and /billing:

  • Interactive Claim Counter: Real-time + button to increment completed claims against assigned batch targets with auto-save.
  • Report Blocker: Submit alerts for portal downtime or missing demographics to pause SLA timers.
  • My Desk (8-in-1 Portal):
    1. My Attendance: View daily punch timestamps, break durations, and active hours.
    2. Shift Calendar: View monthly assigned shift schedules.
    3. Holiday Calendar: View corporate holidays and apply for floating leaves.
    4. My Leave: Check remaining leave balance and submit leave applications.
    5. My Performance: Review KPI scorecards, target vs actual claim speeds, and appraisal goals.
    6. My Documents: Upload and review verified ID proofs, degrees, and billing certifications.
    7. My Assets: Review assigned laptops, serial numbers, chargers, and accessories.
    8. My Requests (Helpdesk): Submit HR support tickets for payroll, leave, or IT assistance.

10. Quality Assurance (QA) System

Accessible at /qa and /quality-management for users with qa_enabled = True:

  • Claim Inspection Queue: Sample completed batches and verify billing codes, modifiers, and patient data.
  • Error Categorization: Classify errors into Minor, Major, or Fatal/Critical severity levels.
  • Rework Dispatch Loop: Automatically send flagged claims back to the billing executive with corrective instructions.
  • Quality Trend Analytics: Departmental accuracy percentages and common denial root causes.

13. Governance, Audit Logs & System Settings

Accessible at /audit and /settings:

  • Centralized Audit Trail: Logs all critical events (Logins, Profile Updates, Batch Allocations, Payroll Locks, Deletions) with timestamps, actor IDs, IP addresses, and categories.
  • One-Click Action Revert: Restore previous values for supported actions directly from the audit log.
  • CSV Export: Download tamper-evident audit records for HIPAA and SOC 2 compliance reviews.
  • Organization Settings: Configure business name, industry, timezone, currency, and standard working hours.

14. Standard Operating Walkthroughs

Walkthrough 1: Employee Daily Routine

1. Log in via /login.
2. Click [ Check In ] in the top header.
3. Go to My Work Queue (/billing) and increment your claim counter.
4. Report blockers if external portals go down.
5. Use [ Take Break ] and [ Resume ] as needed.
6. Click [ Check Out ] at shift end.

Walkthrough 2: HR Monthly Payroll

1. Open Time & Payroll (/time-payroll).
2. Review base salaries, approved OT, and incentives.
3. Verify unpaid leave deductions.
4. Click Lock Payroll Cycle.
5. Click Generate Payslips.
6. Click Export Bank Disbursement CSV for payment.

Walkthrough 3: Team Lead Allocation

1. Open Shift Roster (/scheduling) to assign weekly shifts.
2. Open Work Allocation (/billing) to create batches.
3. Set target quota, priority, and due dates.
4. Track progression on the Tasks Kanban.
5. Review attendance exceptions.

Walkthrough 4: QA Audit & Rework

1. Open QA Workspace (/qa).
2. Select a completed batch from the queue.
3. Audit billing codes and modifiers against guidelines.
4. Log errors and assign severity.
5. Click Dispatch Rework to return to executive.
6. Re-inspect corrected claim and mark Approved.

15. Troubleshooting & FAQ

  • What is "Session Superseded"?
    EasyTrack allows only one active device session per user account to prevent PHI exposure. Logging in on a second device invalidates the previous session.
  • Can administrators read private chats?
    No. Chats are scoped strictly to conversation participants at the database level. Audit logs record only dispatch timestamps, never message text.
  • How do I change my theme or sound settings?
    Click your profile avatar to choose from 6 accent colors or toggle Dark Mode. Click the speaker icon in the header to toggle notification chimes.
  • How do I correct an attendance punch error?
    Navigate to My Desk ➔ My Requests, select Attendance Correction, enter the date and correct timestamps, and submit for HR verification.
